Attending events, 2023-2026

My game dev journey so far has been blessed with some opportunities to showcase my game and to network with fellow devs. Let's take a look back to what has has happened to Ardeimon these past few years.

Electronic Sports and Gaming Summit (ESGS) 2022-2023

This was my first ever foray to public appearances related to game dev. This was also my first time ever attending a gaming-related event. I was really nervous showing my game to strangers and, honestly, a bit overwhelmed with the bug reports and feedback. Looking back, I've met some of my fondest acquaintances from these events.

On my 2nd ESGS attendance, I won the Globe Gen G award, the first time I've also accepted an award of that magnitude.

Philippine Game Dev Expo (PGDX) 2023-2026

PGDX came as a sort of upgrade to ESGS as an entirely new event with more booths, events and a highlight showcasing local indie games.

As I've gotten used to con-going (or more like con-showcasing), my experience of PGDX has been nice so far. It's a good place to connect with local players, meet some new friends and learn through workshops. I would recommend applying for local devs!

Forgive the lack of variety in the pics. I'm really not much of a picture taker, and I didn't have a lot of time to take some. 

Game Dev Summit (GDS) 2024, 2026

I was luckily invited to the first Game Dev Summit held at the Seawind Resort in Boracay last Feb 2024. This event is more catered to developers, for possible publishing and getting investments. There is a game showcase from various developers, networking events, talks as well as workshops.

Having a booth by the beach (yes, we were just meters away) was amazing. Despite being so near, I never really got to enjoy it. I was busy attending workshops. At least I'm a tiny bit wiser...?

I wasn't able to attend the 2nd GDS held at Boracay again last 2025 but made it to the 3rd one, this time in Clark, Pampanga at Hilton Clark Hotel. Instead of a separate booth, Craggenrock was included in the DTI Malikhaing Pinoy booth along with other finalists from the Young Creatives Challenge (section below).

Like the first one, I really spent most of my time attending workshops and roaming around.

These pics were mostly taken early morning of the first day before the event even began, or the night when people were away. There were really a lot of attendees, I just didn't take much photos!

Young Creatives Challenge, Season 1, 2024 (Defense and Finals)

I got into the finals of the first season of Young Creatives Challenge, game dev category last 2024. It's a nationwide contest across different creative categories. We scored 2nd place (or 1st runner-up, can't remember the exact title)! It's my highest career highlight so far.

LikhaSanay Game Dev Masterclasses, 2024

The game dev masterclass focused on game design. We were comprised of a good, small selection of people involved in the game dev scene here in the Philippines, from indie devs and studio reps to the academe. (I love to learn, you can probably tell by now.)

I don't have a lot of pictures of the actual classes because, if I remember correctly, we were not allowed to record or take pictures. There were some that were published publicly but I cannot find them anymore, unfortunately.

In retrospect

Game devs can have fun outside their games every once in a while. As someone who hasn't been part of the industry that long, I find it the whole experience welcoming and warm. I admit there have been multiple times I've felt I'm almost too isolated, but with the company of fellow devs and seeing interested players in these events, I'm reminded that is not the case. At this point, I'm not too sure if I'll be ever graced with similar opportunities in the future but I'm hoping to at least drop by and say hi to some of my newly-found friends from these events, even just through DMs.

If you're a budding game dev or if you just feel a little bit alone, try to bring yourself to events like these as much as you can. It might seem a bit daunting at first, but soon enough you'll feel like you rightfully belong.
